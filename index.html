About

A translator between business and build.

I'm a Business Informatics Professional based in Frankfurt, completing an M.Sc. in High Integrity Systems while working at Air Liquide Global E&C Solutions. What I care about: clarity over complexity, data you can trust, and shipping things that make someone's Monday easier.
